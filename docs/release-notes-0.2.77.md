## Vertical keyboard scrolling

- Scroll normal pages with W/S or the up/down arrows, including an opened artwork, search, Following, recommendations, saved works, Favorites, creator profiles and settings.
- Short presses move smoothly; holding a key continues scrolling with a bounded animation queue. Reduced-motion settings use immediate movement.
- Keep W/S and the vertical arrows for image changes inside Space preview. Text fields, selectors, menus and video controls keep their own keys.
- Scroll nested panels and dialogs without moving the page behind them. Cancel pending movement when changing tabs, opening preview, scrolling with the mouse or leaving the window.
