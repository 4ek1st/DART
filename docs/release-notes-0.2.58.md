## NSFW filters and video sound

- Right-click NSFW to enable Q and NSFW separately. The choice is saved and applies to NSFW mode across Home, Search, Recommendations, Following and artist profiles.
- Each catalog requests and filters the chosen ratings. Following keeps separate preview caches and its existing seen state. Turning both ratings off stops loading and shows an empty-state message.
- Added Settings > Appearance > Video playback > Open videos with sound. The choice applies to artwork pages, quick preview and fullscreen; hover previews stay silent.
- Video sound defaults persist across restarts and artwork navigation. Manual mute choices survive rerenders and signed media URL renewal.
