## Viewer shortcuts and stable results

- Use B to add or remove the current work's bookmark, and A/D to browse previous
  and next artworks. Existing arrows and F for likes remain available. Physical
  keys work across keyboard layouts, and text fields and menus retain control.
- Browse from a focused video in Space preview without closing it first. Native
  Space playback remains available, and old player resources are released when
  switching to another work.
- Keep rendered cards throughout the current results after viewing, liking or
  bookmarking, including scrolling away and returning. New searches, filter
  changes and explicit refreshes apply known-work hiding. Incoming known copies
  stay excluded or merge into their original card.

## Recommendation signals and recovery

- Exclude technical file properties and broad catalog vocabulary from automatic
  taste signals. Require recurring independent evidence as a collection grows,
  while keeping explicit priorities, manual tag choices and small histories useful.
- Retry temporary source failures at their original page with bounded backoff.
  Successful sources and visible cards are preserved; access errors wait for
  settings or an explicit retry. Recovery status is available in all three languages.
