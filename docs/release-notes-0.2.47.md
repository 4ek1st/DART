## Feed personalization

- Renamed Previously viewed to Feed personalization and added Show works by
  followed artists, with English, Russian and German labels.
- Turn the new option off to hide followed creators from search, illustrations,
  recommendations and related works. Following, likes, bookmarks, history and
  artist profiles remain available.
- The option starts enabled and is saved in the local profile. Confirmed creator
  identities and catalog aliases are matched across grouped copies. Cards already
  displayed remain on the current page when following an artist.
