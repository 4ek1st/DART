## Keep known works out of discovery and repeat browsing

- Apply the viewed and saved filter to Following, including confirmed catalog
  copies. Keep cards already displayed on the current page in place.
- Preserve newly learned duplicate identities while browsing with Space or the
  artwork viewer. Skip unvisited works recognized as already viewed or saved.
- Persist identities learned from an opened work's details so known copies stay
  hidden after the work leaves the recent-history list or the app restarts.
- Keep previous-view navigation available and retain pagination after closing
  the original feed tab.
- Prevent a later catalog upload of the same displayed work from promoting its
  old card as New. Fresh independent works still receive their normal New mark.
- Continue through pages containing only known works and exclude hidden works
  from New counts and displayed-work bookkeeping.
