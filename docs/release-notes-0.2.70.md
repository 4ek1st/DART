# Tag image previews

- Hover over a tag to see a small example image. Matching previously viewed works take priority, including available dated history; otherwise the first eligible search result supplies the preview.
- Previews work on artwork tags, Home favorites, recommendation tags, Favorites and search suggestions, with keyboard focus support and English, Russian and German captions.
- A short hover delay, bounded caches, background scheduling and cancellation avoid unnecessary requests. Content, creator, artwork and rating filters still apply; hovering does not record a view or change likes.
- Stable previews survive background page updates. Moving away, scrolling, changing context or opening a viewer dismisses them. Video examples use their thumbnails without playback.
