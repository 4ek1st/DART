## Smoother browsing and faster Favorites

- Search keeps unfinished text, selection and focus while results load. Each tab keeps its own search draft.
- The first page can open while additional Favorites collections are still loading. Content filters and hidden-work preferences remain applied before results appear.
- Repeated artwork details no longer rebuild unchanged Liked and Bookmarks collections or reset their caches.
- Related works use confirmed artists and animators across catalogs to improve author variety.
- Favorites renders the visible portion of large character and tag collections while keeping all available covers accessible in the viewer.
- Faster grouping and result updates reduce pauses when browsing recommendations and loading more artworks. New identities, mirrored copies, image variants and video versions remain checked.
