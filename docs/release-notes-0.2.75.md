# Centered gallery preview

- Space preview keeps the original image size and screen center throughout gallery navigation, loading, errors and first/last pages.
- Narrow fragments of the previous and next images show the exact adjacent frames. They sit flush with the upper and lower window edges, with square corners at the window boundary.
- The existing up/count/down controls form a compact vertical panel to the left of the image. Narrow windows keep the controls accessible without shifting the artwork.
- W/S use physical key positions and work with English, Russian and German layouts, including Caps Lock. Text fields, modified shortcuts and composition retain their normal behavior.
- Adjacent images reuse the existing media cache with at most two neighboring images prepared. Inactive videos stay unloaded; current pixels, retry handling, zoom/pan, A/D, likes, bookmarks and return-to-card behavior are preserved.
