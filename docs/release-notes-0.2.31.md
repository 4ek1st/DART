## Global curation

- Curation is now controlled only in Settings > Curation. Removed the separate
  Curated mode from the catalog; New and Popular remain available.
- The selected factors apply across Home, catalog and search results, Following,
  artist profiles, Liked, Bookmarks, history and the creator's other works.
  Recommendations and related works continue to include curation in their
  relevance scores, preserving explicit tag priorities.
- New works stay first in Following. Background updates and additional pages
  preserve the positions of cards already shown. Arrow navigation follows the
  same order as the artwork grid.
- Factor changes rerank cached views without additional catalog requests.
  Disabling curation restores the original order without rewriting saved lists.
- Existing Curated tabs migrate to their previous New or Popular source
  ordering. Curation factors and unrelated profile settings are preserved.
- Updated the settings descriptions in English, Russian and German.
